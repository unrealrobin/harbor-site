This covers developers who use Harbor and visitors to harborlauncher.com.

**It does not cover players.** When a studio uses Harbor in its game, we handle player data on that studio's instructions. The studio decides what is collected and is responsible for telling players about it. For questions about player data, contact the developer of the game you are playing.

## What we collect

Retention periods are maximums. We may delete sooner.

| What | Why | Legal basis | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name, email, sign-in details | Create and operate your account | Contract | Account lifetime, then up to 12 months |
| Your projects and configuration | Provide the service | Contract | Account lifetime, then up to 30 days |
| Billing and subscription records | Take payment; tax and accounting | Contract; legal obligation | 7 years |
| Emails you send us | Answer you; support history | Legitimate interests | Up to 24 months |
| Server logs, including IP | Keep the service running; prevent abuse | Legitimate interests | Up to 30 days |
| Website usage: pages, referrer, clicks | Understand how people use harborlauncher.com | Legitimate interests | Up to 12 months |

**We never see your card details.** Stripe handles payments; your card number goes to Stripe directly. We receive only a token, the last four digits, and whether the charge succeeded.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or buy information about you.

## Who we share it with

Service providers acting on our instructions: **Clerk** (sign-in), **SurrealDB** (database, on AWS), **DigitalOcean** (hosting and storage), **Resend** (waitlist storage), **PostHog** (website analytics).

**Stripe** acts as an independent controller of payment information, not on our behalf; its own privacy policy applies.

We may also disclose information if legally required, or in connection with a sale of the business.

## Where it is stored

The **United States**. If you are in the EEA or UK, that is a transfer outside your region. We rely on Standard Contractual Clauses, as do our providers.

## Cookies

The Harbor dashboard uses only cookies needed to sign you in. On harborlauncher.com we use PostHog analytics, which sets cookies. You can block these cookies in your browser at any time without losing functionality.

## Your rights

Email **privacy@harborlauncher.com** to get a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to our use of it, or have it sent elsewhere. We respond within one month.

If you are in the EEA or UK and unhappy with our handling, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

## Changes

We will email you and post a dashboard notice at least 30 days before material changes take effect.

## Related

The binding data protection terms between Paracosm and studios using Harbor, covering player data, are in **Section 12 and Annexes A, B, and C** of the [Harbor Terms of Service](/terms).
